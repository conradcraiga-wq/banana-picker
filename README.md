# Banana Picker

A small web app that reads banana ripeness live from the phone camera and says whether a banana is a Today, Tomorrow or Day-after pick, or a skip.

Everything runs in the browser. The camera video is analyzed on the device and is never uploaded. Training samples are kept in the browser's local storage on that device. The app makes no network requests after it loads and uses no analytics, accounts or third-party scripts.

Add it to the iPhone Home Screen from Safari (Share, then Add to Home Screen) and open it from there.
